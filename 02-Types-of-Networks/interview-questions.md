# 💼 Interview Questions – Types of Networks

## 📖 Introduction

This section contains beginner to advanced interview questions on Types of Networks. These questions are commonly asked in Linux, Networking, Cloud, and DevOps interviews.

---

# 🟢 Beginner Level

## 1. What is a computer network?

**Answer:**
A computer network is a group of connected devices that communicate and share data and resources using networking protocols.

---

## 2. What is LAN?

**Answer:**
LAN (Local Area Network) connects devices within a small geographical area such as a home, office, school, or college.

**Example:** Office network.

---

## 3. What is MAN?

**Answer:**
MAN (Metropolitan Area Network) connects multiple LANs across a city or metropolitan area.

**Example:** A bank connecting all branches in one city.

---

## 4. What is WAN?

**Answer:**
WAN (Wide Area Network) connects networks across countries or continents.

**Example:** The Internet.

---

## 5. What is PAN?

**Answer:**
PAN (Personal Area Network) connects personal devices over a short distance.

**Example:** Bluetooth between a phone and wireless earbuds.

---

## 6. What is WLAN?

**Answer:**
WLAN (Wireless Local Area Network) is a LAN that uses Wi-Fi instead of cables.

**Example:** Home Wi-Fi.

---

## 7. Which network type is the Internet?

**Answer:**
The Internet is the world's largest WAN.

---

# 🟡 Intermediate Level

## 8. What is the difference between LAN and WAN?

**Answer:**

| LAN | WAN |
|-----|-----|
| Small area | Large area |
| Faster | Slower |
| Less expensive | More expensive |
| Private | Public or private |

---

## 9. Which network is the fastest?

**Answer:**
LAN is generally the fastest because devices are connected over short distances.

---

## 10. Which network covers the largest area?

**Answer:**
WAN covers the largest geographical area.

---

## 11. Why is LAN faster than WAN?

**Answer:**
Because LAN has shorter communication distances, lower latency, and higher bandwidth.

---

## 12. Can a WAN connect multiple LANs?

**Answer:**
Yes. A WAN is designed to connect multiple LANs across different locations.

---

## 13. What is bandwidth?

**Answer:**
Bandwidth is the maximum amount of data that can be transmitted over a network in a given time.

---

## 14. What is latency?

**Answer:**
Latency is the time taken for data to travel from the source to the destination.

---

# 🔴 Advanced Level

## 15. Which type of network is used in cloud computing?

**Answer:**
Cloud providers use LANs inside data centers and WANs to connect users across the world.

---

## 16. How do DevOps engineers use networking?

**Answer:**
DevOps engineers use networking while configuring Linux servers, Docker containers, Kubernetes clusters, cloud infrastructure, and CI/CD pipelines.

---

## 17. Which type of network is used in an office?

**Answer:**
LAN.

---

## 18. Which type of network is used in a university campus?

**Answer:**
Usually LANs connected together, and sometimes a CAN (Campus Area Network).

---

## 19. Why is networking important for DevOps?

**Answer:**
Networking enables communication between applications, servers, containers, cloud services, and users. Without networking, DevOps tools cannot communicate.

---

## 20. Explain LAN, MAN, and WAN with examples.

**Answer:**

- **LAN:** Home or office network.
- **MAN:** Network connecting branches across a city.
- **WAN:** Internet connecting users worldwide.

---

# ⭐ Interview Tips

- Explain the concept first, then provide a real-world example.
- Compare LAN, MAN, and WAN when asked.
- Mention how these network types are used in Linux, Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, and cloud environments.
- Use simple language before discussing technical details.

---

# 🎯 Key Takeaways

- Know the definition of each network type.
- Understand the differences between LAN, MAN, WAN, PAN, and WLAN.
- Be able to explain where each network type is used in real-world and DevOps environments.
