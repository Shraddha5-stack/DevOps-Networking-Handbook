# Chapter 37 — Real-World Case Studies — Interview Questions

This chapter focuses on real-world DevOps, networking, troubleshooting, production incidents, and incident-response scenarios.

The goal is not to memorize commands.

The goal is to explain:

```text
Problem
  ↓
Impact
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Investigation
  ↓
Root Cause
  ↓
Fix
  ↓
Verification
  ↓
Prevention
```

---

# 1. Beginner-Level Questions

## 1. What is a real-world case study in DevOps?

A real-world case study describes an actual or realistic technical problem and explains how an engineer investigates, resolves, and prevents it.

---

## 2. What is troubleshooting?

Troubleshooting is the systematic process of identifying the cause of a problem and restoring normal operation.
