# ❓ Network Topologies – Interview Questions

This section contains beginner, intermediate, advanced, and scenario-based interview questions to help you prepare for networking and DevOps interviews.

---

# 🟢 Beginner Level

## 1. What is a network topology?

**Answer:**  
A network topology is the physical or logical arrangement of devices and communication links in a network. It defines how devices are connected and how data flows between them.

---

## 2. Why is network topology important?

**Answer:**  
It affects network performance, scalability, reliability, maintenance, and troubleshooting.

---

## 3. Name the six common network topologies.

**Answer:**

- Bus
- Star
- Ring
- Mesh
- Tree
- Hybrid

---

## 4. Which topology is most commonly used today?

**Answer:**  
Star Topology.

---

## 5. Which device is commonly used in a Star Topology?

**Answer:**  
A network switch.

---

# 🟡 Intermediate Level

## 6. What is the difference between Physical and Logical Topology?

**Answer:**  
Physical topology shows how devices are physically connected, while logical topology shows how data travels between devices.

---

## 7. What is Bus Topology?

**Answer:**  
A topology where all devices share a single backbone cable.

---

## 8. What is Mesh Topology?

**Answer:**  
A topology where devices are connected to multiple or all other devices, providing multiple communication paths.

---

## 9. Why is Mesh Topology highly reliable?

**Answer:**  
Because if one connection fails, data can use an alternative path.

---

## 10. What is Tree Topology?

**Answer:**  
A hierarchical topology that combines multiple Star Topologies.

---

# 🔴 Advanced Level

## 11. Which topology is best for enterprise networks?

**Answer:**  
Hybrid Topology, because it combines the advantages of multiple topologies.

---

## 12. Why is Star Topology preferred over Bus Topology?

**Answer:**

- Better performance
- Easier troubleshooting
- Higher reliability
- Easy expansion

---

## 13. Which topology has no single communication path?

**Answer:**  
Mesh Topology.

---

## 14. What happens if the switch fails in Star Topology?

**Answer:**  
All connected devices lose communication.

---

## 15. What happens if the backbone cable fails in Bus Topology?

**Answer:**  
The entire network becomes unavailable.

---

# 💼 Scenario-Based Questions

## 16. Your office network suddenly stops working. The central switch has failed. Which topology is likely being used?

**Answer:**  
Star Topology.

---

## 17. Which topology is commonly used in cloud data centers?

**Answer:**  
Hybrid and Mesh Topologies.

---

## 18. Which topology is easiest to expand?

**Answer:**  
Tree Topology.

---

## 19. Which topology provides the highest fault tolerance?

**Answer:**  
Mesh Topology.

---

## 20. As a DevOps Engineer, why should you understand network topologies?

**Answer:**  
Because they help design scalable infrastructure, troubleshoot connectivity issues, understand cloud networking, and manage communication between servers, containers, and Kubernetes clusters.

---

# ⭐ Interview Tip

> Don't just memorize the names of topologies. Explain **how they work**, **where they are used**, **their advantages and disadvantages**, and **why one topology is chosen over another**. Interviewers value practical understanding.
